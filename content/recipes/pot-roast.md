#### Ingredients
- 3-4 lb chuck roast
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 large onion, sliced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 bell peppers (red and yellow), sliced
- 8 oz mushrooms, quartered
- 1 cup dry red wine
- 2 cups beef broth
- 2 tablespoons tomato paste
- 2 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 3 sprigs fresh thyme
- 2 bay leaves
- 1 cup kalamata olives, pitted
- 1 can (14 oz) artichoke hearts, drained
- Salt and fresh black pepper to taste

#### Instructions

##### Prep the Meat
- Pat roast dry and season generously with salt and pepper
- Let sit at room temperature for 30 minutes

##### Sear
- Heat olive oil in large Dutch oven over medium-high heat
- Sear roast on all sides until deep brown (about 4-5 minutes per side)
- Remove and set aside

##### Build Flavors
- In same pot, sauté onions until translucent (5-7 minutes)
- Add garlic, cook 1 minute
- Add mushrooms and peppers, cook 5 minutes
- Stir in tomato paste, cook 1 minute

##### Braise
- Add wine, scrape bottom of pot
- Add broth, herbs, return roast to pot
- Bring to simmer, cover, and transfer to 250°F oven
- Cook 4-5 hours until fork-tender
- *Note: For slightly faster cooking, can use 275°F for 3-4 hours*

##### Finish
- Add olives and artichokes last 30 minutes
- Remove herbs before serving
- Let rest 15 minutes before slicing

#### Serving Suggestions
Serve with crusty bread to soak up the rich sauce, or over polenta/risotto.

#### Tips for Success
- The roast is done when you can easily twist a fork in the meat with little resistance
- If not tender enough at the suggested time, continue cooking in 30-minute intervals
- Keep the lid tight during cooking to retain moisture
- For best results, choose a well-marbled chuck roast
- Don't rush the searing process - a good brown crust equals more flavor

*This Mediterranean twist adds brightness and depth beyond traditional pot roast while keeping it hearty and satisfying.*
